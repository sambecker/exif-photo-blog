# Photo recipes

Terms for the camera settings that define the look of a photo.

## Language

**OM recipe**:
The camera settings that define an OM SYSTEM or Olympus photo's color or monochrome look. Recipes describe the settings used for a photo, without a user-assigned name.
_Avoid_: Profile name, numbered profile

**Color recipe**:
An OM recipe for a color look, including adjustments to individual colors.

**Mono recipe**:
An OM recipe for a monochrome look, including its color filter and film grain settings.

**Profile slot**:
A numbered place in the camera for saving a set of profile settings. The slot number does not identify the recipe used for a photo.
_Avoid_: Recipe identity

**OM recipe gallery**:
A collection of photos whose OM recipes have matching settings. Color and mono recipes belong to separate galleries even when their shared controls match.

**Matching OM recipes**:
Recipes with the same kind and active profile settings, including selected white balance and its adjustments. Matching excludes profile slots, exposure compensation, measured Auto white-balance results, inactive settings, and camera processing preferences such as Noise Filter and Gradation.

**Incomplete OM recipe**:
A recipe with a missing or uninterpretable defining setting. It has no public recipe control or membership in a recipe gallery.
